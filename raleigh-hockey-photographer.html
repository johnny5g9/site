Raleigh booking questions

What to know before you inquire.

How far ahead should Raleigh games be booked?

Weekend dates can fill quickly, so two to three weeks of lead time is recommended when possible. Last-minute availability depends on the schedule.

Can coverage prioritize one player?

Yes. Share the jersey number, likely line combinations, and the moments you care about before the game.

Is travel outside Raleigh available?

Yes. Cary, Wake Forest, Garner, Morrisville, and nearby Triangle markets are available depending on the date and event schedule.
